# Payroll input processing

Accept payroll summaries, payslips and EPF, SOCSO, EIS or PCB payment proofs. Treat payroll documents as confidential source evidence. Reconcile gross pay, employee deductions, employer contributions, net pay and statutory payment proofs without exposing employee-level detail unnecessarily. Use a miscellaneous document only when needed to represent a supported cash movement; use reviewed journals for supported payroll accounting. Never invent employee data, statutory rates or payment confirmation, and keep incomplete payroll packs blocked for review.
