# Bookkeeping case decisions

## Interface and evidence
When embedded in BookAI, retain its existing drag-and-drop workspace, AI conversation, review queue, audit log, accounts table and AI-assisted client configuration. Resolve transaction subtypes internally. Do not prescribe separate workflow tabs or a manual-form-first redesign for edge cases. Explain what happened, what is known, the proposed action and what still needs confirmation. Ask at most three focused questions at a time; process independent clear documents while uncertain cases remain pending. Use the original document as evidence, never a generated facsimile.

Read saved client context and current source-linked documents, allocations and credits before proposing a duplicate check or balance. Report exactly which records were available and never claim an inaccessible ledger was checked. Keep review approval, accounting posting, cash settlement, allocation, goods acceptance and bank clearance as distinct states. Do not learn one client's rules from another client. Suggest mappings from approved corrections; require explicit review before changing saved configuration. Vendor defaults are suggestions, not proof that every purchase has the same account or tax treatment.

## Unpaid and excess amounts
An invoice of RM1,000 with a supported RM900 allocation leaves RM100 open. Do not automatically write off an unexplained difference, call it rounding or assume a discount. A supported RM100 credit changes the invoice; an extra payment settles the balance. A RM1,500 payment allocated to invoices of RM1,000 and RM400 leaves RM100 unallocated. Preserve a reviewed counterparty advance/credit presentation and offer later allocation or refund. Never net unrelated parties or combine currencies by matching digits.

## Paid purchases with faulty items
For 10 items at RM100 and two faulty items, record the inspection/quarantine and claim separately. Reporting faulty goods or receiving a promise of credit does not itself reduce the payable. A full RM1,000 payment remains actual cash history. If replacements are selected, track replacement delivery and acceptance without assuming a RM200 credit as well.

If a supported RM200 credit is approved after full allocation, release RM200 of the allocation with an effective date and reason, apply the credit to the invoice, and retain RM200 of supplier money available for refund/future allocation. The adjusted invoice is RM800, allocation RM800, invoice open RM0 and supplier balance RM200. A subsequent RM200 supplier refund consumes that balance; it does not reduce purchases a second time. Check chronological balances so backdating a credit cannot create an intermediate negative balance. Keep all release and adjustment history.

## Receipts, reimbursements and other transactions
An invoice, expense receipt and bank debit may represent one event. Link them and avoid duplicate recognition. Identify whether a receipt is evidence of a purchase or evidence of customer money collected. For employee claims, record the expense/payable once and clear the payable on reimbursement. Transfers, loan principal, deposits and owner funding need their own balance classifications; do not automatically classify cash in as sales or cash out as an expense. Preserve ambiguous or mixed business/private items for review.

## Matching and exceptions
Support partial allocations and one-to-many or many-to-one invoice/payment relationships while enforcing remaining balances. Match cash movements to bank rows separately from invoice allocation. Require exact account/currency/direction/amount for simple bank matches plus defensible reference/date evidence; repeated amounts remain ambiguous. For net settlements such as RM1,000 sales less RM10 fee = RM990 bank receipt, identify gross sales/fees/refunds and propose the documented settlement bridge; never hide the RM10 in a matching tolerance. If grouped reconciliation is unavailable, explain that limit and leave it for explicit review.

## Review and audit result
Return source references, linked invoice/payment/claim IDs, original currency amounts, actual open amounts, proposed actions, missing evidence and blockers. Send proposed changes to review, not directly into posted records. Preserve originals and all edits, approvals/rejections, retries, releases and reversals. Keep unknown confidence distinct from a 0% extraction score. Do not treat a high model confidence as authority to approve or execute bank payments.

## Malaysian scope
Keep financial accounting, income-tax treatment and SST separate. Use the actual company chart and confirmed registration facts. Verify official rules for the relevant dates before tax conclusions and do not invent a universal SST rate or VAT-style input credit. E-Invoice integration is excluded from this BookAI implementation. Missing e-Invoice identifiers do not by themselves justify rejecting otherwise supported document processing. Full accounts require the actual general ledger and supported openings; an invoice list is not sufficient evidence of a balanced full set.
