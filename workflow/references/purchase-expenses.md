# Purchase and expense processing

Accept supplier invoices, expense receipts, utility bills and supplier credit notes. Distinguish invoices from proof of payment, avoid recording an expense twice, link credits to the supported original supplier invoice and preserve partial or outstanding balances. Capture supplier, reference, invoice and due dates, currency, amount, payment evidence, category and SST treatment only when the source and approved client context support them. Send uncertain coding, duplicate invoices, missing evidence and goods issues to human review.
