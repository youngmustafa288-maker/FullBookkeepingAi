# Staff claims and petty cash processing

Accept staff receipts, claim forms and mileage or travel claims. Capture claimant, business purpose, source date, amount, currency, category and payment or reimbursement status. Separate an employee claim from the later reimbursement, avoid duplicates against company-card or supplier records, and keep private or unsupported portions pending. Mileage and travel claims require the actual approved basis and supporting journey details; never invent a rate or tax treatment.
